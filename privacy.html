Privacy Policy — Dark Files

Last updated: October 14, 2026

"Dark Files" is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel CLASSIFIED | ألغاز وملفات. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to manage that owner's own channel only: uploading the channel's videos and thumbnails, scheduling them, adding them to the channel's playlists, and posting or editing the channel's own comments on its own videos.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or of any other user.
  • It does not access any account other than the channel owner's.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising.

Data handling

The only credential is the owner's authorization token, kept encrypted and private. The owner can revoke it at any time from Google Account permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Through the YouTube channel above.
